Settings
Beep
Short tone on touches, cards, and when a timer starts, pauses or runs out.
Vibration
Haptic feedback on the same actions. Needs System Haptics enabled in your device settings.
Épée passivity timer
Épée only. A 1:00 no-touch clock runs with the main timer and resets on every touch. When it hits zero you are prompted to give P-cards (FIE t.124).
Include P-Yellow warning
Off: P-Red then P-Black (rule from 1 Sep 2026). On: P-Yellow, P-Red, P-Red, P-Black (earlier rule). Check your competition's rules.
